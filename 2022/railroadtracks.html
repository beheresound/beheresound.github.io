Railroad Tracks in the Woods

Back

10/19/2022

I have things to do but this is where I belong:
On railroad tracks in the woods!

I have people to meet and songs to write,
But nothing to do on railroad tracks in the woods!

Just sitting with my legs crossed is always enough.
No one pretends on the tracks in the woods!

Breathe in and imagine you are still water
Reflecting how things really are
