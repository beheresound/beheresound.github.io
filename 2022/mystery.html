Mystery Crowns the Jewel

Back

2022

Mystery crowns the jewel
Head down ears open
But the pen moves
alert, I am a person of the
world more than I have ever been
when I hide away
and listen closely to my own rambling incoherencies
This time hammering into the humidity...
Multitudes
Trying so hard to sell your greatest work
I am incapable of love
But I am obsessed with red illumination:
Leaving myself an intricate puzzle to decipher
and figure out just what was going on,
I condemn myself to an irrational reality.
