8/23/25

experimenting with reflections... I've been reflecting all my life but never methodically, never with a goal in mind... all my poetry has been idle entertainment "tear-jerkers" pleasure jaunts and time-killers... so... let's see...

Today is 8/23/25 and I am now committing to improving myself in every way until I can fit the universe in my consciousness-cup. fill it up and still have empty space to spare.

Continue to work on evenmindedness in all categories. Today and yesterday I spent considerable time visualizing Guru in my forehead. Keep it up!

I feel His presence growing stronger within me every minute.

I keep letting my calmness slip out with plans and ideas and frustrations and my phone. Give it all up! There is only Guru.

The goal is final liberation in this lifetime. Read the lessons and practice methodically. Apply the calmness of meditation to all situations. No more "double-life" letting the restless mind take over between meditations. You cannot resist the pull! Give up, now!

Meditate unceasingly
Study methodically
Apply what you've learned
Give it all up to God

Aum. Peace. Amen.

8/24

Reflection: I am consistently unsatisfied... one way or another. I accomplished some of my greatest dreams and I'm still lost as ever. Slowly, Guru is convincing me the only way to lasting happiness is through Him and by finding God. Remember: There is only one way!

Distractions: It's not gonna get you anywhere. Give it up! Sadness overhwelmed me... I turned toward my Guru and my studies.

8/25

Tune yourself

Life force is the hidden link between consciousness and matter. You can feel the life force drawing downwards and out and dissipating itself in the forgetfulness, sacrilegious Maya i.e. thinking anything in the world will bring you happiness. I won't. Give up trying. See what happens when you retain the life force. Don't squander it on idle time wasting and long past nostalgia... Bring it inwards, upwards, and Godwards. Remember: Keep yourself to the highest standard of living.

I am not this body
I am immortal soul
bliss, ever new joy

Be that!

Aum Peace Amen.
