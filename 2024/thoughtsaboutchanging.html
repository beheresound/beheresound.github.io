Nothing changing at home

Woke up today to the rain on my face and I thought it was pretty wondered how'd it up end up this way? It's May, it's dreary but I feel just fine and my friends are all golden and living their lives it feels like we all made it to some other side but nothing has changed just what's in our minds our bodies are growing we learned how to stretch and treat ourselves right and clean up the mess in our rooms and clear tables and take out the trash it's the first day I'm home where it doesn't feel like a crash I was fine a few days ago I'll be fine all the rest cause I woke up today in a bed of wet grass. Nothing has ended all par for the course but how I feel mended from years of remorse, can I feel mended from years of remorse? And now pouring rain I'm inside I am dry and yes, it's true, I have nothing to say but words still come out and it's better that way to let truth unravel instead of think it away- So now I have no plans just keep my room in good shape and unpack my things and put it away in all the right places so I know where to stay (in the center of the room, open space, and content to watch the orange light infuse into my skin as the rain pours down, down, down into new gutters outside of my window where I used to sit same as now 'cause nothing's changed)

Nothing changing in Ithaca

Cause nothing's changed I'm back in the same place under fluorescence and pictures still leaning on the wall unplaced untouched since 2017 I made those on Adobe Photoshop in another brightly lit room in the high school - pictures I made with no effort and probably high-strung definitely annoyed and not caring at all but here they remain time capsules leaning on the wall in another undecorated Costco furniture, Holy Bible grandma mailed in one Christmas leather-bound on the side table apartment. And my high school graduation photo taped to the fridge. I thought- maybe these places would fizz themselves out - it's their house, not mine, I say and if they want to come up for air that's just fine and here I'll be- Ah! You've come but that's untrue, of course it's shared and how come under fluorescence I'm suddenly a little kid again and my stomach hurts and I lash out and bite and beat myself up for the pain I've inflicted? It feels so familiar yet so old and can't we just move on from this? It's not up to them and it's not up to me either but I hope soon one of us (the whoever it is that's being waited on by the other) will snap out of their daze and open us into essence refute this melodramatic theme this cosmic repetition which is dry and bland because I know there's so much more to this and why can't we see it yet? I hope we see it yet and (by the way, Marty thought this was sweet - the pictures, they're sweet) maybe all we have to do is say wouldn't it be nice to have a soothing lamp in here? or a candle or let's clear the space, clear this decades old maybe who knows how old cycle and come out of our damn shells hatch into the greater but the pictures still lean against the wall in my dad's apartment and it's true I never put in the effort in the first place maybe he knows it.
