The guts of it all
It's just in listening:
This makes good and bad go
away
Judgment ceases and
eternity opens
like at the gates of the cemetery,
and golden lights showcasing
a dead one

Flower’s sacrifice is wilting
to a name that is forgotten
by me, right after I read it
I don’t remember, I just listen
It passes right through me
