8/29/24

I wonder if you could tell me what it means when the beauty of everything is what kills motivation? Is it simply that - end of case - the beauty of everything is what kills motivation? It kills time, it kills the doer, melt away and drown in reverb, opaque enjoyer enjoys no more. Still the lingering tendril of passion is who asks this question: what does it mean when the beauty of everything is also its unbecoming? I allow it - there, I said it - universe of my creation, unfold! Be only beauty, let there be no witness, none shall remain to bask in the quietude - that passion seeker is demanding and wails like a baby. Goodnight, goodnight, tucked away baby go to sleep - night remains with or without your crying. In fact, cool city, lonely empty town, humidity and reflection of orange street lamp at night - these slangy allegories are all beset with the image of my non-appearance - and the poignancy of dissolution as I enter the scene. Of course, everything that's beautiful is far away - and it kills.
