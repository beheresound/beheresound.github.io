The river runs through my house again
Saints of all religions...
O Radha, Radha, Radha

I am incorporated metronomic humanity
I am a ruined carpet in the monastery
River of life
Sweep clear tantric neumona
establish once and forever a
crown to place my love for the world
And bow down to her
the greatest illusion of all
O Kali, Kali, Kali!
I bow to Thee everywhere
in the world fully but not of it-
where else does anything come from?

I am not this little wave of life
I am the cosmic ocean of bliss from
which all things are made manifest-

Om
