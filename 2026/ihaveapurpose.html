7/12/26

My Lord will lead me to freedom
Well, good, I have a purpose
The same purpose I've had for a million lifetimes
The same purpose God planted in me as the first
embryonic human. to say Hi and smile
and wave
as the panopticon of eternity revolves
infinitely around finite changing forms
from one to two to five to twelve to
8 billion. In the calm center of unmanifestation,
I am.

What a fascinating panorama
of birth and death and joy and suffering
and all glued together with desire
Thy will, which upholds infinite worlds
Aummmmmmmmm
Thy will which upholds my own and all
my desires
(traps, or, a seatbelt in the caurosel)
To stay grounded, hah! in mortality
As you wish Lord
let my life's narrative unfold as Thy will
I will be a good actor, acting
in accordance with the Guru's baton
Woooooow! As it is written the
Cosmic Play unfolds.
