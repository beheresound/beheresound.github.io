Hello, Myself
Indifference and reverence are
one and the same
Glancing over my shoulder
at the setting sun,
I am In-amored
with the sound of
my boots crunching snow
and how it reverberates through my toes.
I sit down in the warmth surrounded by leather
& become myself
and giggle. Cosmic Author
and I, subjective infinitely
and infinitely subject, trace fingertips
(ecstasy, definitely, for a second)
then vow to a century of celibacy.

PATIENCE - is indifference and reverence
in awe of an astounded aphorism
just out of reach...
