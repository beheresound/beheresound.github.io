4/17/26

My peace is impenetrable
my love is the love beating
in the hearts of all
beings everywhere

I am the rainstorm that
shatters humid april late
afternoon
I am all the cars on the road
driving through it
and all the people astounded

I am the bloody snare drum and a
broken hand
I'm reconcilliation and dissaray
I am a man of action
and a renunciate, and all
this
form
and essence. I am the
Dharma King Yuddhisthira and I
have been in exile for 13 long years.
patience weathered,
long hair and 2 eyes
gazing up

I reclaim my rightful
throne of Spirit.

I am Spirit and
all of its manifestations
in the world.

Spirit is, nothing else is
the real substance.

Spirit is, I know it not

I dissolve
little wave
into the ocean
of bliss

Shake, stir, misconstrue
change, morph
changeless, you are
changeless and beginningless
and eternal is the Spirit.

Verily I say this unto Thee Amen
